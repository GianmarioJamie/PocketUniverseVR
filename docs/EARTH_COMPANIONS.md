# Earth neighbourhood

Moon, ISS and Hubble appear while Earth is held and during its smooth return to orbit. They follow Earth in the same scene. Select the actual model to open its information in the side card. The inspecting hand keeps holding Earth; use the other hand to select a companion. Since 0.6.2 there are no floating companion labels, bottom selection tabs or neighbourhood caption; Earth's name is hidden while it is held. Discover Moon in the guided journey remains an alternative for a single hand or mouse. That journey keeps the neighbourhood visible during its first discovery.

## Data sources

- Moon: https://science.nasa.gov/moon/facts/ — approximate diameter, mean Earth distance, orbital period and synchronous rotation.
- ISS: https://www.nasa.gov/missions/station/spot-the-station-frequently-asked-questions/ — roughly 90-minute orbit, low Earth orbit and role of the station.
- Hubble: https://science.nasa.gov/mission/hubble/overview/about-hubble/ and https://science.nasa.gov/mission/hubble/overview/faqs/ — roughly 95-minute orbit, launch year and observatory role.

Values are educational approximations. Animation does not predict current satellite positions. Distances, orbit periods and spacecraft dimensions are deliberately adapted to fit around a held Earth. The Moon's size relative to Earth follows its approximate diameter ratio; spacecraft are exaggerated for visibility.
