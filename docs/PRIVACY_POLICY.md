# Pocket Universe VR — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026.

Pocket Universe VR is provided by Jamie, using the Meta developer organization Jamie_Joe. Contact: johnmario.lombardi@gmail.com.

## Data used during the experience

The application processes headset position and orientation, hand joint positions and hand interaction signals locally to render the experience and let you select and move objects. These tracking values are used during the session and are not intentionally recorded, transmitted to the developer, or used to identify you.

This version has no account system, advertising, analytics service, purchases, multiplayer or network permission. It does not access microphone audio, eye tracking or raw camera images. Planet information and illustrative models are included in the application.

## Local files and retention

The engine and operating system may retain local operational logs and application cache. The app does not intentionally write hand or head poses to those logs. We do not receive those local files unless you choose to share diagnostic material with us. Diagnostic material you send may include your email address and any information you include; we use it to respond to your request and investigate the issue, and retain it only as needed for that purpose.

The developer does not maintain a server-side profile or tracking history for this application. Session interaction state is discarded when the app closes.

## Deletion and contact

You can remove local application files by uninstalling the app or using any app data controls available on your headset. For privacy questions or to request deletion of diagnostic material you previously shared, email johnmario.lombardi@gmail.com.

## Meta platform services

Meta supplies the device operating system and tracking services. Its own processing is governed by Meta's privacy policies. This policy describes this application's behavior; it does not replace the platform's policy.

## Changes to this policy

We will update this policy before adding telemetry, cloud services, accounts, purchases, recordings or any new use of device or user data. The effective date above identifies this version.
